Hospital Operations & Patient Flow Dashboard
Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze hospital patient activity, operational performance, clinical case characteristics and revenue patterns.

The dashboard provides insights into patient volume, length of stay, case severity, specialty activity, payer composition and revenue contribution to support data-driven hospital management.

Key Insights
Total Cases: 500
Total Revenue: Approximately 9M
Average Length of Stay: 1.28 days
Average Revenue per Case: 17.05K
High-Severity Cases: 16.8%
Highest-Volume Specialty: General Surgery with 70 cases 		
Other High-Volume Specialties: Orthopedics (68) and Emergency (66)
Payer Composition: Insurance accounts for approximately 85.6% of cases, while Corporate accounts for 14.4%
Revenue by Specialty: General Surgery and Orthopedics are among the highest revenue-contributing specialties
Revenue Trend: Monthly revenue fluctuates considerably, with the highest levels occurring around June/July and lower levels later in the year


Recommendations
High-Volume Specialties: Monitor workload and resource requirements in General Surgery, Orthopedics and Emergency due to their high case volumes.
Length of Stay: Investigate specialties with higher average lengths of stay to identify opportunities to improve patient flow and capacity utilisation.
Clinical Complexity: Pay attention to specialties with higher concentrations of severe cases when considering resource and operational planning.
Revenue Drivers: Monitor high-revenue specialties and assess whether their financial contribution is aligned with their patient volumes.
Payer Concentration: Continue monitoring the hospital's payer composition and its relationship with revenue generation.
Revenue Trends: Investigate significant monthly changes in revenue to understand the operational or patient-volume factors contributing to these fluctuations.
